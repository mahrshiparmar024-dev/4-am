05 · Main letter

To the Person I Once Called Home

There are people we meet for a season, and then there are people who quietly become a part of the way our heart beats.

You were the second kind.

I still remember the version of me that believed distance was only a number. I believed that love could survive time zones, bad days, missed calls, and thousands of kilometers—as long as two people kept choosing each other.

So I chose you.

On ordinary mornings.

On difficult nights.

On days when life felt heavy.

When everyone else was asleep and only my screen knew your name, I chose you.

I never counted the hours we spent talking, the times I waited, or the little surprises I made for you. Those GitHub pages, those words, those late-night ideas... they were never obligations. They were simply my way of making distance feel a little smaller. Every page I built carried one quiet hope—that somewhere, you'd smile because someone, far away, was thinking about you.

Maybe that's why goodbye feels so loud.

People think heartbreak begins when two people say goodbye.

I don't think it does.

Heartbreak begins the first morning you wake up and instinct tells you they're still yours... until reality gently reminds you they are not.

The world keeps moving.

The sun still rises.

People still laugh.

Coffee still gets cold.

Everything looks exactly the same.

Yet somehow, your own little universe feels unbelievably empty.

The hardest part isn't that you're gone.

The hardest part is that I still catch myself wanting to tell you about small things.

A song I'd know you'd love.

A meme that would've made you laugh.

A sunset I'd instinctively take a picture of...

...before remembering there is nowhere left to send it.

It's strange how love works.

It isn't built on grand gestures.

It grows through ordinary moments.

A "Good morning."

A "Did you eat?"

A random photo.

A late-night conversation that somehow makes tomorrow feel lighter.

Those little moments quietly become a home.

And when that home disappears...

Even ordinary things begin to hurt.

I used to believe that love meant never giving up.

Now I think love is also knowing when someone's heart has already left, even if their words haven't.

There is no point in blaming you anymore.

Maybe people simply outgrow the promises they once made.

Maybe forever sometimes lasts only until life changes.

I don't know.

And perhaps I never will.

But if there is one thing I hope you never question, let it be this:

You were loved.

Not in loud, dramatic ways.

But in quiet ones.

In every page I built when no one asked me to.

In every paragraph I rewrote because I wanted it to be perfect for you.

In every notification that made me smile.

In every prayer where your happiness somehow came before mine.

In every future I imagined, where you already had a place without ever asking for one.

You were loved with the kind of love that expected very little...

Except honesty.

Today, I don't write this to ask you to come back.

I don't write this to change your mind.

Love that has to convince someone to stay has already forgotten what love is.

I write this because every story deserves an ending written with the same sincerity as its beginning.

You once became my safest place.

Then, without warning, you became the lesson I never asked to learn.

And despite everything...

I don't hate you.

Hate would mean carrying you forever in the form of anger.

I'd rather carry you as gratitude.

Gratitude for every laugh that was genuine.

For every memory that made distance disappear.

For reminding me that my heart was capable of loving someone so completely.

You changed me.

Not because you stayed.

But because, for a while, you made me believe in forever.

Maybe forever was never meant to be measured by years.

Maybe forever is measured by how deeply someone changes your soul after they're gone.

If that's true...

Then a part of you will always live quietly within mine.

Not as regret.

Not as bitterness.

Not even as a wound.

But as proof...

That once, in this enormous universe,

I loved someone with everything I had.

And I have no regrets for loving you that way.

One day, years from now, you might forget the messages, the calls, the dates, even the reasons why our paths separated.

But I hope you never forget one simple truth.

Somewhere in this world, there was a boy who looked at a glowing screen late at night, smiled because your name appeared, and believed he had found home.

Maybe we weren't meant to last forever.

Maybe our story was only meant to teach us something about love, trust, and letting go.

If that's the case...

Thank you.

Thank you for every smile that was real.

I'm sorry for every dream that reality couldn't protect.

I sincerely hope life gives you everything you've ever wished for.

And if we ever become strangers again...

I hope we smile.

Not because we forgot each other.

But because we remember that, for one beautiful chapter of our lives,

we loved honestly.

Goodbye, my once-home.

Take care of yourself.

I'll take care of the memories.

Thank you for being one beautiful chapter of my life.
